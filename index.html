DISCOVER ANCIENT WISDOM & HEALING ARTS

Reiki 1st Degree

This two-day, in-person seminar includes approximately 10 hours of instruction. Reiki Level 1 establishes a foundation for cultivating greater self-awareness, relaxation, inner peace, and deeper understanding of personal well-being. It also prepares participants to progress to Reiki Level 2 and continue their personal and spiritual development through the practice of Traditional Reiki healing.

Learn Now

Reiki 2nd Degree

Reiki Level 2 is designed for students who have successfully completed 21 days of self-Reiki practice following the completion of Reiki Level 1. This course supports practitioners in developing distance healing skills, fostering personal growth through guided Reiki meditations, and cultivating a deeper understanding of the inner self.

Crystal Seminar

This course is designed for 2nd degree Reiki students who wish to deepen their connection with crystals which increases the energy vibrations multiple times and explore traditional healing practices.
