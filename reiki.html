About Reiki and Its History

Discover the universal life force energy that heals body, mind, and soul.

Understanding Reiki

Reiki is a Japanese word where "Rei" means universal life force and "Ki" means energy. It is this energy with which we are born and live throughout the life. This Universal life Force Energy is pervasive throughout the entire Universe and has the potential to heal all living beings and even non-living things. Reiki makes a person healthy by affecting his all the three aspects i.e. Body, Mind and Soul.

Reiki is not a Religion. It has nothing to do with calling Spirits, Black magic or Hypnosis. Any person whether educated or uneducated, of any profession can learn Reiki. The founder of Reiki was from Japan. He imparted this education of Reiki to the people and established a Reiki channel. Shortly before his death, Dr. Usui gave this responsibility to carry forward this method of Reiki healing to his Disciple Dr. Chujiro Hayashi. In 1938, Dr. Hayashi granted Mastership to Takata Hawayo and made her a Reiki Master. In India, the knowledge of Reiki was introduced by Dr. Poula Horan in 1986 and Dr. Karanpal Singh Sandhu was the one to become the first Reiki Master in the whole North India. Due to its simplicity, the knowledge of Reiki is gaining popularity all over the world and the number of people learning Reiki is increasing day by day.

IMPORTANT NOTE

Reiki healing can be done from distance but it is not possible to learn Reiki from distance or over the internet (because initiation process which is performed by a true Reiki Master is possible only if Reiki learner is physically present). Beware of such wrong persons who claim to teach Reiki from distance or over the internet.

What Reiki Is Not

REIKI is not a religion, as it holds no creed or doctrine. It has nothing to do with spiritualism or occult in any shape or form. It has nothing to do with the calling up of ghosts or demons, nor is it concerned with hypnosis or any other kind of psychological technique. To practice REIKI no special kind of faith or belief is required. In REIKI treatment, use is made solely of a natural but concentrated form of cosmic energy.
